    About Us

    A new trading card shop that buys, sells and trades.

    GuimaRares & Holos opened in 2026 for people who love trading cards. We carry singles, booster boxes, booster bundles and sealed product across the games people play most, along with accessories and custom-made pieces like deck boxes, caddys and bulk boxes.

    What we stand for

    Fair deals. We quote clear prices, whether you are buying a single or selling a whole collection.

    Honest condition. We describe cards as they are, so what arrives matches what you saw.

    Real help. Looking for a specific card? Tell us and we will help track it down.

    Work with us

    Want to sell or trade? See Sell & Trade. Want something made just for you? See Custom Products. Anything else, contact us.

    FAQ

    Quick answers to common questions.

    Do you have a physical store?

    Not at this time currently we are online only.

    What do you sell?

    Singles, booster boxes, booster bundles and sealed product for Pokémon, One Piece, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Riftbound and other popular games. We also sell accessories and custom products.

    How do I buy something I see in the shop?

    Send us a message on the Contact page with what you want, and we will confirm availability and next steps.

    How do you describe card condition?

    Each single is listed with a condition such as Near Mint or Lightly Played. If a card does not match its description, see our return and refund policy.

    Do you buy collections?

    Yes. Send us a list on the Sell & Trade page, and photos on Instagram if you have them and we will reply with an offer.

    What is the difference between cash and trade credit?

    Cash is paid to you for your cards. Trade credit goes toward anything in the shop, and it pays more than cash.

    Can you make a custom deck box, caddy or bulk box?

    Yes. Share your idea on the Custom Products page, and send photos on Instagram. We build a 3D model and a quote for your approval before making anything.

    Can I return something?

    In many cases, yes. Our Returns & Refunds page has the details.

    Are you affiliated with the game publishers?

    No. We are an independent shop. All game and card names belong to their owners.

    I cannot find the card I am looking for.

    Ask us on the Contact page. We can search for it.

    Returns & Refunds

    Last updated November 13, 2026

    The short version

    If something is not right, tell us through the Contact page and we will make it right. Here is how it works for each kind of purchase.

    Sealed product

    You can return sealed product within 14 days of delivery if it is unopened, unaltered and in its original condition.

    Singles

    If a card does not match the condition we listed, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will offer a refund or a replacement. Cards must be returned in the same condition we sent them.

    Accessories

    Unused accessories in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

    Custom products

    Custom pieces are made to order from a design you approve, so they cannot be returned or refunded. If your piece arrives damaged or does not match the approved design, contact us within 7 days and we will fix it or refund you.

    Damaged or wrong items

    If your order arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will replace it or refund you, and we cover return shipping.

    How to start a return

    Contact us with your name, what you ordered and the reason. We will reply with return instructions. Please do not send anything back before we confirm.

    Refunds

    Once we receive and check your return, we refund the item price to your original payment method. Refunds usually take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank. Unless the return is our error, shipping costs are not refunded and return shipping is paid by you.

    Cancellations

    Contact us before your order ships and we will cancel it for a full refund.

    Cards you sell or trade to us

    Offers are confirmed after we inspect your cards. If you decline a revised offer, we will return your cards.
